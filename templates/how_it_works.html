Educational AI Prototype

How HealthLens Works

Understanding the AI-assisted analysis process

1

You Enter a Health Claim

Start by typing or pasting any health claim you want to understand better. This could be something you read online, heard from a friend, or saw in an advertisement.

↓
2

HealthLens Sends It to Gemini AI

Your claim is securely sent to Google's Gemini language model, which generates an educational analysis based on the instructions provided by HealthLens.

↓
3

AI Analyzes the Claim

The AI examines the wording, context, and educational considerations behind your claim. It identifies important factors that would help you think critically about whether and how to trust the information.

↓
4

Response is Formatted

HealthLens converts the AI response from Markdown into clean, readable HTML with proper formatting, headings, and lists. This makes the analysis easy to scan and understand.

↓
5

You Receive an Educational Report

Your report includes:

  • Claim Category — The type of health claim (nutrition, treatment, etc.)
  • Context — Relevant background information to understand the claim
  • Points for Critical Evaluation — Key considerations for evaluating the claim
  • Questions to Explore — Thoughtful questions that help you dig deeper
  • Further Sources — Types of evidence and reliable resources to learn more
  • Key Takeaway — A summary of the most important educational message

Important: Critical Thinking, Not Verification

HealthLens teaches you how to think about health claims, not what to think about them. The goal is to encourage thoughtful evaluation of health information so you can make more informed decisions.
