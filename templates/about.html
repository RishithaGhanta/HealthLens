About HealthLens

AI-Assisted Health Literacy Prototype

Our Mission

HealthLens helps improve health literacy by encouraging users to think critically about health claims instead of accepting health information at face value. We believe that understanding how to evaluate health information is a valuable skill for everyone.

What HealthLens Does

HealthLens provides structured analysis that:

  • Identifies the claim category (nutrition, treatment, prevention, etc.)
  • Provides educational context about the topic
  • Highlights important considerations for critical evaluation
  • Suggests thoughtful questions to explore
  • Recommends reliable sources for further learning

What HealthLens Does NOT Do

It's important to understand the limitations of HealthLens:

  • Does not diagnose diseases — It cannot and should not be used for medical diagnosis
  • Does not replace healthcare professionals — Always consult qualified doctors for medical concerns
  • Does not determine if claims are true or false — It teaches you how to evaluate claims yourself
  • Does not provide medical advice — It is an educational tool only

AI Transparency

HealthLens uses Google's Gemini language model to generate educational analysis. The responses are created based on the model's general knowledge and reasoning capabilities. This means the analysis reflects what the AI has learned from training data, not verified scientific conclusions. All responses are intended for educational purposes to help you think more critically about health information.

Disclaimer

HealthLens is an AI-assisted educational prototype developed to promote health literacy. The analysis is generated using a language model's general knowledge and reasoning capabilities. It is intended to support learning and critical evaluation and should not be interpreted as a definitive scientific assessment or as a substitute for advice from qualified healthcare professionals.
